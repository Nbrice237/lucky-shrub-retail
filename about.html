About us

Our story

Based in Tucson, Arizona, Lucky Shrub is a medium-sized garden design firm that specializes in garden design and creation, maintenance and landscaping. The company also runs a small plant nursery that sells indoor and outdoor plants.

Lucky Shrub was started by a husband and wife team, Jason and Maria. Jason is the garden architect who creates and oversees all designs and Maria manages all the marketing for the company and oversees the nursery.
